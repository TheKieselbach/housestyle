# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

Concretely: no harassment, no personal attacks, no dismissiveness towards
people who are new to this. Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.
Disagreement is fine and useful; contempt is not.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
belonging to this project.

If something needs addressing, open a private security advisory or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled discreetly.
Persistent or serious breaches lead to a ban.

The maintainer decides these questions and is answerable for them. There is no
committee; this is a small project.
